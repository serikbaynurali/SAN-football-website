Welcome to SAN Football Analytics Platform

Your comprehensive digital archive for professional football match statistics, modern tactical developments, international club rankings, and athlete profiles curated by Team SAN.

Core Analytic Pillars

Our platform focuses on four main analytical pillars that drive modern association football:

  • Tactical Systems: Deconstructing positional play, pressing triggers, and defensive transitions.
  • Performance Metrics: Expected goals (xG), progressive passing volume, and ball recovery zones.
  • Tournament Schedules: Tracking UEFA Champions League and domestic league fixtures.
  • Athlete Evaluation: Deep dives into elite forwards, midfielders, and defenders.
